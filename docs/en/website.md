# Web control

> **Platform note:** Web control is available on **Windows** and **macOS** only. It is **not available on the Apple TV edition**.<br>
> Under Apple’s App Store policies, apps that embed a full web browser are generally difficult to ship, so this capability is provided on the desktop editions only.

Web control lets you open a video website on the computer connected to your TV, then operate it from your phone. You can browse and play without walking over to a keyboard and mouse.

![Phone controlling online video on the computer](/screenshot-online-video.png)

In the English interface, the built-in options include YouTube and Netflix. You can also save personal websites on this TinyPlay computer; every phone on the same local network can open them.

Sign in on the computer yourself, and follow each service's rules for accounts, memberships, regions, and supported devices.

## How to use it

1. Open **Website library** on your phone and choose a site.
2. The site opens on the computer connected to the TV.
3. Switch to **Remote** on your phone to play, pause, seek, or enter and exit fullscreen.

Saved personal websites use the generic remote, including **Select page element** for tapping buttons on the page. Site-specific shortcuts appear only for built-in services.

## Notes

Web control turns ordinary browser actions on the computer into a remote that is easier to use from the sofa. It does not change what each service already allows: what you can watch, and whether ads appear, still depends on the account and subscription signed in on the computer.

If a website redesigns its player, some controls may need a later update. TinyPlay does not remove ads, bypass sign-in, membership, device, or regional restrictions, or provide downloading, recording, or rebroadcasting.
